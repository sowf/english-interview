# 2-Refine: Own Your Stories

**Goal:** Make the story bank YOURS. This is about execution, not content creation.

## ⚠️ Important Mindset

**The bank is a starting point, not the final answer.**

In 1-draft, AI helped generate polished stories. But those are AI's words, not yours.  
In 2-refine, YOU work on the bank until you can tell each story naturally.

**Why scores might be low at first:**
- Reading AI-generated text ≠ owning the story
- You need to rewrite sections in YOUR voice
- Practice until phrases feel natural, not memorized
- The goal: tell the story as if it happened to you (it did!)
- **Sometimes the story itself doesn't work** — if it feels forced or weak after 3-4 practices, consider replacing it with a different experience from your career

## How to Practice

1. **Edit your bank** `learning/2026-story-bank.md` — rewrite in your words
2. **Read aloud daily** — 2x through all stories
3. Run `2-refine-practice` — get feedback on execution
4. **Iterate** — update bank based on what feels unnatural

## What We Evaluate (Execution Focus)

| Area | Weight | What We Check |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Delivery | 45% | Fluency, STARR structure, confidence |
| Content | 35% | Story depth, metrics, authenticity |
| Seniority | 20% | Ownership, strategic thinking, leadership signals |

## When to Move to 3-Mastery

- [ ] Each story practiced 5+ times
- [ ] Can tell any story without reading
- [ ] Scores consistently 70+/100
- [ ] Vocabulary from bank feels natural
