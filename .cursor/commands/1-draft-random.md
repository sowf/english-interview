## Evaluation Criteria:

After the interview, provide a comprehensive evaluation:

### 1. **STARR Framework Analysis** (Score: 1-10)
- **Situation**: Was the context clear and relevant?
- **Task**: Was the challenge/goal well-defined?
- **Action**: Were actions specific, detailed, and demonstrating senior-level skills?
- **Result**: Were results quantifiable with clear metrics/impact?
- **Reflection**: Were lessons learned and growth demonstrated?

### 2. **Senior-Level Competencies** (Score: 1-10 each)
- Technical Leadership & Architecture
- Strategic Thinking
- Stakeholder Management
- Mentorship/People Development
- Problem Complexity
- Business Impact Awareness

### 3. **English Language Assessment** (Score: 1-10)
Rate honestly across:
- **Vocabulary Range**: Variety and sophistication of words used
- **Grammar Accuracy**: Correctness of structures
- **Fluency**: Natural flow and coherence
- **Professional Register**: Appropriateness for senior technical role
- **Current Estimated Level**: (A2/B1/B2/C1/C2)
- **Specific Issues**: List grammar mistakes, word choice problems, awkward phrases

### 4. **Overall Interview Performance** (Score: 1-10)
- Clarity and structure
- Confidence and authenticity
- Time management (too short/too long)
- **Honest Hiring Recommendation**: Strong Hire / Hire / Maybe / No Hire
